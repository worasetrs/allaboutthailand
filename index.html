Culture & etiquette

How to be a good guest

Short guides on the wai, temples, the monarchy, Buddhism, Thai food and the language.

Culture guides →

Do

  • Cover shoulders and knees when visiting temples
  • Take your shoes off before entering homes and shrines
  • Stand for the royal anthem before films
  • Keep calm and smile in a disagreement

Don't

  • Touch someone's head or point your feet at people
  • Speak disrespectfully about the royal family; it is a crime
  • Vape: e-cigarettes are illegal in Thailand
  • Climb on or pose disrespectfully with Buddha images
