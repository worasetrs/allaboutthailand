Thailand is preparing a flat **450-baht fee** for foreign visitors, the same amount whether they arrive by air, land or sea. On 9 October the Deputy Prime Minister ordered agencies to work out how the fee would be collected, how the money would be spent and how the process would be kept transparent.

**What it means for you:**

- **Nothing changes yet.** You do not pay any entry fee today.
- The government hopes to start collecting the fee in 2027, after public consultation, Cabinet approval and publication in the Royal Gazette.
- Early reports say air arrivals would be charged first, with land and sea later. Treat that timeline as tentative.

We will update this story when the fee is approved.
