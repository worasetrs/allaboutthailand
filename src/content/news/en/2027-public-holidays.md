The official 2027 public holiday calendar has been published. Government offices, including immigration offices, are closed on these days. Plan visa extensions and 90-day reports around them.

**Long weekends in 2027:**

- **20–22 February:** Makha Bucha (Sunday 21 Feb) with a substitution day on Monday 22 Feb.
- **13–15 April:** Songkran falls Tuesday to Thursday.
- **18–19 July:** Asalha Bucha and Buddhist Lent Day.
- **23–25 October:** Chulalongkorn Day falls on a Saturday, with Monday 25 Oct off.
- **5–6 December:** Father's Day falls on a Sunday, with Monday 6 Dec off.

See every date on our [holiday calendar](/calendar/). Dates can change if the government adds special holidays.
