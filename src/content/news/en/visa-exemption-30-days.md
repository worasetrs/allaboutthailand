Thailand's 60-day visa exemption, introduced in July 2024 for 93 countries, ended on 15 September 2026. Four Ministry of Interior notifications published in the Royal Gazette on 31 August replaced it.

**What it means for you:**

- Ordinary passport holders from 60 countries and territories can now stay **up to 30 days** without a visa.
- Visa-exempt entry is now for **tourism only**. Business meetings, training and urgent work need a visa.
- Land-border entries under the exemption are limited to two per calendar year, with exceptions for Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and Singapore.
- Travellers who entered on or before 14 September keep the stay they were given.

If your country is no longer on the list, apply for an e-Visa before you travel unless a bilateral agreement covers you.
