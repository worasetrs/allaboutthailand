Preliminary figures from the Ministry of Tourism and Sports show **24,423,680 foreign arrivals** from 1 January to 17 October 2026, down 4.12% on the same period last year. Visitors spent about 1.19 trillion baht.

**Top markets so far this year:**

| Country | Arrivals |
|---|---|
| China | 4.14 million |
| Malaysia | 3.10 million |
| India | 1.84 million |
| Russia | 1.33 million |
| South Korea | 0.90 million |

In the week of 11–17 October, long-haul arrivals rose 10.6% as school holidays began in Europe and the Americas. The ministry expects numbers to keep rising as the cool season starts.
