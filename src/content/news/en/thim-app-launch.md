Thailand's Immigration Bureau officially launched its **THIM** app on 25 September 2026, after a testing period. The app has been available since 1 August on the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery, and has been downloaded more than 600,000 times.

**What it means for you:**

- THIM lets you fill in your **Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)** on your phone. It is the same card, so you do not need to file twice.
- THIM is **optional**. The official TDAC website, tdac.immigration.go.th, still works.
- Immigration says it will add appointment booking, extensions, 90-day reporting and Visa on Arrival to the app later. No dates have been given, so keep using the current methods for now.

How to fill in the card: [TDAC guide](/guides/tdac-arrival-card/).
