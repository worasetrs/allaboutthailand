## Two common routes

- **Non-Immigrant O-A (long stay):** applied for from your home country. It requires a criminal record check, a medical certificate and Thai health insurance.
- **Non-Immigrant O, then a retirement extension:** enter on a Non-O visa, then apply in Thailand for a one-year "extension of stay based on retirement".

## Financial requirements for the yearly extension

You must show one of these:

- **800,000 THB** in a Thai bank account, held for at least 2 months before you apply and kept above the required level during the year;
- a monthly income of at least **65,000 THB**, shown with an embassy income letter or Thai bank deposits; or
- a **combination** of bank deposit and annual income totalling 800,000 THB.

How long the money must stay in the account after approval varies by immigration office. Ask your local office for its current checklist before you go.

## Each year

1. Apply for the extension at your local immigration office within 30 days before your permission to stay expires.
2. File [90-day reports](/guides/90-day-report-and-tm30/) between extensions.
3. Get a **re-entry permit** (1,000 THB single, 3,800 THB multiple) before travelling abroad, or your extension ends when you leave.

You cannot work in Thailand on a retirement visa.
