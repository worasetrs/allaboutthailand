## Who it is for

- **Workcation:** remote workers employed by a company outside Thailand, digital nomads and freelancers with foreign clients.
- **Thai soft power:** people joining activities such as Muay Thai training, Thai cooking courses, medical treatment, seminars or music festivals, with a letter from the provider.

Your spouse and children under 20 can apply as dependants.

## Main requirements

- Aged 20 or over and **outside Thailand** when you apply.
- Proof of funds of at least **500,000 THB** (or the equivalent in your own currency). Most embassies ask for bank statements covering several recent months. Check your embassy's exact rule.
- Proof of remote work (employment contract, or client contracts and a portfolio) or an acceptance letter for your soft power activity.

## How long you can stay

The visa is valid for five years with multiple entries. Each entry gives you 180 days, and you can extend once by another 180 days at an immigration office for 1,900 THB.

## What you cannot do

The DTV does **not** allow you to work for a Thai company, invoice Thai clients or get a Thai work permit. If you stay in Thailand for 180 days or more in a calendar year, you may become a Thai tax resident. Get tax advice about foreign income you bring into Thailand.

You also need to file [90-day reports](/guides/90-day-report-and-tm30/) during long stays.
