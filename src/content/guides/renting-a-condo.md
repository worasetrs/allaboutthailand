## Lease terms

- A standard lease is **12 months**, with a deposit of **2 months' rent** and the first month paid in advance.
- Shorter leases of 1–6 months are available at a higher monthly rent.
- Get the lease in English (or bilingual) and keep a copy of the owner's ID and title deed.

## Before you sign

1. Photograph every room and any damage, and attach the photos to the lease.
2. Ask what electricity rate you will pay. Some landlords charge more than the official electricity authority rate.
3. Confirm who pays for internet, water, the building's common fee and repairs.
4. Ask the landlord to file your **TM30** and give you the receipt. See [TM30 explained](/guides/90-day-report-and-tm30/).

## Can foreigners buy property?

- Foreigners can own a **condominium unit** in their own name, as long as foreign ownership in the building stays within 49% of the total floor area and the money is transferred from abroad.
- Foreigners generally **cannot own land**. A registered lease of up to 30 years is a common alternative.
- Get independent legal advice before buying.
