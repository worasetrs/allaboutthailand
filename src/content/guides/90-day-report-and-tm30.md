## 90-day report (TM.47)

Anyone staying in Thailand for more than 90 consecutive days, on any long-stay visa, must tell immigration where they live every 90 days. The count restarts each time you leave and re-enter Thailand.

**When:** from 15 days before the due date until 7 days after it.

**How:**

1. **Online** at the Immigration Bureau's TM.47 site. Your first report after each entry usually has to be made in person.
2. **In person** at the immigration office for your province, with your passport, a completed TM.47 form and copies of your passport photo page, visa and latest entry stamp.
3. **By registered post**, sent at least 15 days before the due date.

A late report costs a fine of 2,000 THB. If immigration finds you have not reported at all, the fine can be up to 5,000 THB.

## TM30 (notification of residence)

TM30 is filed by the **owner or manager** of the place where you stay (your landlord, hotel or host) within 24 hours of your arrival there. Hotels do this automatically.

If you rent a condo or house, ask your landlord for a copy of the TM30 receipt. Immigration offices often ask for it when you extend a visa or file your first 90-day report. Make sure a new TM30 is filed when you return from a trip abroad or move.
