## From the airports

- **Suvarnabhumi (BKK):** the **Airport Rail Link** runs from the basement to Phaya Thai in about 26 minutes (maximum fare 45 THB), with connections to the BTS and MRT. Public taxis leave from level 1. Expect the meter fare plus a 50 THB airport surcharge and expressway tolls.
- **Don Mueang (DMK):** the SRT Red Line runs to Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal, which connects to the MRT.

## Trains

- **BTS Skytrain** and **MRT** cover central Bangkok and are the fastest way to travel at rush hour. Fares are about 17–65 THB.
- You can pay by contactless Visa or Mastercard at MRT gates. Otherwise buy single tickets from machines, or a Rabbit card for the BTS.

## Taxis and apps

- Ask for the meter (**"poet mee-toe duay"**, see [phrases](/essentials/#phrases)). Flag fall is 35 THB. If a driver refuses, take the next taxi.
- **Grab** and **Bolt** show the price before you ride and are widely used.

## Tuk-tuks and boats

- Agree on the price before you get in. A tuk-tuk is a fun ride but rarely cheaper than a taxi.
- The **Chao Phraya Express Boat** is a cheap, scenic way to reach the Grand Palace, Wat Arun and Chinatown.

## A common scam

If a stranger tells you the Grand Palace or a temple is closed today and offers a cheap tuk-tuk tour, walk away. The "tour" ends at gem or tailor shops.
