## Hospitals

Large private hospitals in Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai and Pattaya have English-speaking staff and international patient centres. Public hospitals are much cheaper but busier, and English may be limited.

Private hospitals usually ask for a deposit or proof of insurance before treatment. **Travel insurance with medical cover is strongly recommended**, especially if you plan to ride a motorbike.

## Pharmacies

Pharmacies are everywhere, and pharmacists can advise on minor problems. Some medicines that need a prescription at home are sold over the counter; others need a prescription in Thailand too.

## Health risks

- **Dengue fever** is spread by mosquitoes, mostly in the rainy season. Use repellent day and night.
- **Heat:** drink plenty of water and avoid the midday sun from March to May.
- **Air quality:** PM2.5 levels are often high from January to April, especially in the north. Check the AQI on our [home page](/).
- **Road safety:** Thailand's roads are among the most dangerous in the world. Wear a helmet, and check that your licence and insurance cover motorbikes.
