## What changed

The 60-day visa exemption that started on 15 July 2024 has ended. Four Ministry of Interior notifications, published in the Royal Gazette on 31 August 2026, replaced it from **15 September 2026**:

- Ordinary passport holders from **60 countries and territories** can stay **up to 30 days** without a visa.
- The exemption is for **tourism only**. Business meetings, training and urgent work are no longer covered.
- Nationals of **Mauritius and Seychelles** get a separate 15-day exemption.
- **Visa on Arrival** now covers only Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia, at designated checkpoints.

The list includes the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, India, Taiwan, South Africa and most EU countries. Always confirm your nationality on the official list before you travel.

## If you entered before 15 September 2026

You keep the stay that was stamped on entry. A 60-day entry granted on or before 14 September remains valid for its full period.

## Countries with their own agreements

Some countries have bilateral visa-waiver agreements with Thailand, and their rules come from that agreement rather than the new list. Chinese passport holders, for example, enter under the Thailand–China mutual visa exemption agreement. Check with the Thai embassy in your country.

## Extending your stay

You can extend a visa-exempt stay **once**, by up to 30 days, at a Thai immigration office:

1. Go to the immigration office for the province where you are staying before your permitted stay ends.
2. Bring your passport, form TM.7, one passport photo (4 × 6 cm), proof of your address in Thailand and a copy of your passport pages.
3. Pay the 1,900 THB fee in cash.

## Land border entries

Visa-exempt entries over land borders are limited to **two per calendar year**. Nationals of Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and Singapore are excepted.

## If you need more than 30 days, or you are not a tourist

- **Long holiday:** apply for a Tourist Visa (TR) through the [Thai e-Visa website](https://www.thaievisa.go.th/) before you fly.
- **Remote work or long stays:** see the [Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)](/guides/destination-thailand-visa/).
- **Business meetings or work:** get the right Non-Immigrant visa (usually Non-B) in advance.
- **Retirement:** see the [retirement visa guide](/guides/retirement-visa/).

Overstaying is fined 500 THB per day, up to 20,000 THB, and long overstays can lead to a ban from re-entering Thailand.
