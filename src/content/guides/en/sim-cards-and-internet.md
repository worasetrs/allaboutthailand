## The networks

Thailand has two main networks, **AIS** and **True** (which merged with dtac in 2023). Both have strong 4G and 5G coverage in cities and tourist areas.

## Buying a SIM

- **At the airport:** operator counters in the Suvarnabhumi, Don Mueang, Phuket and Chiang Mai arrival halls sell tourist SIMs with data for 8 to 30 days.
- **eSIM:** AIS and True sell tourist eSIMs online before you fly, which you activate on landing.
- **In town:** operator shops and 7-Eleven sell SIMs and top-up cards.

You need your **passport** to register a SIM. This is required by law. The shop scans your passport and may take a photo.

## Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is common in hotels, cafés and malls. Avoid logging into banking apps on open networks.
