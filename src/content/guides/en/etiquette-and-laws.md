## The monarchy

The Thai royal family is deeply respected. Insulting the King, Queen, Heir-apparent or Regent is a serious crime under Section 112 of the Criminal Code, with prison sentences of 3 to 15 years per offence. This includes social media posts. Stand when the royal anthem plays before films in cinemas.

## Temples

- Cover your **shoulders and knees**. Remove your shoes before entering a temple building.
- Do not touch, climb on or pose disrespectfully with Buddha images. Buddha images may not be taken out of Thailand without a permit.
- Women should not touch monks or hand things to them directly.

## Everyday manners

- The **wai** (palms together, a small bow) is the traditional greeting. Return one when you receive one. You do not need to start one with shop staff.
- The head is considered sacred and the feet low. Do not touch people's heads, and do not point your feet at people or Buddha images.
- Staying calm matters. Raising your voice in a disagreement usually makes things worse.

## Laws that surprise visitors

- **E-cigarettes and vapes are illegal** to import, sell or possess. Tourists are fined and arrested.
- **Cannabis** is a controlled herb. Since June 2025, buying it requires a prescription, and smoking in public can lead to fines.
- **Drones** must be registered with the authorities before you fly them.
- Carry your **passport** or a copy, and show it to police if asked.
- Alcohol sales are restricted at certain hours and banned on Buddhist holy days and election days.
- Gambling outside the state lottery is illegal.
