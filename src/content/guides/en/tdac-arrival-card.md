## What it is

The TDAC replaced the paper TM.6 arrival card on 1 May 2025. It is required for arrivals by air, land and sea. It is **not a visa**: you still need a visa or visa exemption to enter.

## Who needs one

- Every non-Thai traveller, including babies and children. Each person needs their own card.
- You need a new TDAC for **every entry**.
- Transit passengers who do not pass through Thai immigration do not need one.

## How to fill it in

1. Open the official site, **tdac.immigration.go.th**, within the 3 days before your arrival date.
2. Enter your passport details, flight or vehicle number, and the address where you will stay first.
3. Add your travel history for the last 14 days, which is used for health screening.
4. Submit. You receive a confirmation by email. Save it to your phone or take a screenshot.

The TDAC is **free**. Any website that charges a fee is not the official one.

## The THIM app

The Immigration Bureau officially launched the **THIM** app on 25 September 2026 (App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery). You can submit your TDAC through it instead of the website. It is the same card, so do not file twice. Using the app is optional.

The Immigration Bureau plans to add 90-day reporting, extensions and appointment booking to THIM later. Until those go live, keep using the current methods described in our [90-day report guide](/guides/90-day-report-and-tm30/).
